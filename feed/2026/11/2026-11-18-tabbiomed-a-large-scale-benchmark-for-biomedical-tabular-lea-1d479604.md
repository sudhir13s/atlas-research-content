## Summary

The authors introduce TabBioMed, a large-scale benchmark of 94 curated public biomedical tabular datasets spanning multiple domains. The framework unifies these datasets for reproducible evaluation across classification, regression, and multi-target tasks, benchmarking 23 models including classical baselines, tree models, neural architectures, and tabular foundation models. The results indicate that while foundation models deliver strong predictive performance, tree-based models and tuned multilayer perceptrons remain highly competitive depending on resource constraints and the data regime.
