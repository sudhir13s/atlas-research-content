## Why It Matters

Applied masked autoencoding to video by masking 90-95% of spatiotemporal patches and reconstructing them, learning powerful video representations with minimal compute. Showed that video is even more redundant than images, enabling extreme masking ratios. The leading self-supervised pretraining method for video understanding tasks like action recognition.
