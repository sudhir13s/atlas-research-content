## Why It Matters

Used one language model to automatically generate adversarial prompts that expose failures in another, scaling red-teaming beyond what human testers can cover. Found that LLM-generated attacks surface novel failure modes humans miss. Established automated red-teaming as a standard practice in AI safety evaluation.
