## Why It Matters

An LLM agent that plays Minecraft by writing code, maintaining a skill library, and designing its own curriculum of progressively harder tasks. Demonstrated that agents can accumulate reusable skills and self-improve without human intervention. A compelling proof-of-concept for autonomous, open-ended agent learning in complex environments.
