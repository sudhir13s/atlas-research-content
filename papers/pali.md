## Why It Matters

Jointly trained a 17B vision-language model across 100+ languages on web-scale image-text data, excelling at OCR, visual QA, captioning, and scene-text understanding across languages. Demonstrated that scaling vision-language models to multiple languages simultaneously improves all languages rather than trading off between them. A key reference for building globally useful multimodal systems.
