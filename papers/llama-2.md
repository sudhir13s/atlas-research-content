## Why It Matters

Introduced Grouped Query Attention and extended context to 128K tokens while being openly licensed for commercial use. The accompanying safety fine-tuning paper set the standard for responsible open releases. Kicked off the modern open-weight era where startups could build real products on top of a frontier-class base model.
