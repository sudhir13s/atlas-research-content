## Why It Matters

Fine-tuned BERT with siamese networks to produce fixed-size sentence embeddings that can be compared with cosine similarity, reducing semantic search from hours to milliseconds. Created the sentence-transformers library now used in virtually every embedding-based application. The practical foundation of modern vector search and RAG pipelines.
