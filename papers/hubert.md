## Why It Matters

Learned speech representations through an offline clustering step followed by masked prediction, outperforming wav2vec 2.0 on several benchmarks. The features capture phonetic, speaker, and prosodic information without any labeled data. Widely used as a feature extractor for downstream speech tasks including voice conversion and emotion recognition.
