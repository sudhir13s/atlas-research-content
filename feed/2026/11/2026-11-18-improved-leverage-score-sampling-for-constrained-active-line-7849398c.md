## Summary

The paper studies active learning for constrained linear regression and proposes ellipsoid-regularized leverage scores to bias sampling toward directions important for the constraint geometry. The authors prove that this method improves query complexity by depending on an anisotropic effective dimension rather than full dimension, and demonstrate lower regression error in experiments.
