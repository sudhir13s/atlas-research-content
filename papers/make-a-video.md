## Why It Matters

Generated videos from text descriptions by leveraging image-text pairs and unlabeled video separately — no paired text-video data needed. Used a cascade of spatial and temporal generation stages. Showed that the scarcity of text-video training data can be circumvented with clever decomposition, opening video generation to labs without massive proprietary datasets.
