## Summary

The authors propose ReflectScene, an MLLM-guided closed-loop diffusion framework for text-conditioned 3D indoor scene generation. The method incorporates hierarchical prior-grounded scene understanding, MLLM-guided semantic reward evaluation, and decision-conditioned trajectory-consistent diffusion to correct object layouts. Experiments on 3D-FRONT demonstrate improved functional consistency and semantic alignment.
