## Why It Matters

Introduced the Q-Former — a lightweight transformer that bridges a frozen vision encoder to a frozen LLM using learned query tokens. This two-stage pretraining approach (vision-language representation learning, then vision-to-language generative learning) efficiently connects any vision encoder to any LLM. The architectural blueprint that most subsequent open VLMs adapted.
