## Why It Matters

Demonstrated that training a reward model on human comparisons and optimizing against it with RL produces summaries humans significantly prefer over supervised baselines. The first compelling evidence that RLHF works for language tasks. Directly paved the way for InstructGPT, ChatGPT, and the entire RLHF alignment paradigm.
