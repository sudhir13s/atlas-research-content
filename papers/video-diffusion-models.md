## Why It Matters

Extended the diffusion framework from images to video by introducing a space-time factorized U-Net that jointly denoises spatial and temporal dimensions. The first paper to demonstrate that diffusion models produce temporally coherent video. Established the architectural template — spatial layers plus temporal attention — that every subsequent video diffusion model builds upon.
