## Why It Matters

Distilled BERT into a model 40% smaller and 60% faster that retains 97% of BERT's language understanding capability. Proved that knowledge distillation works for transformers at scale and that much of a large model's capacity is redundant for downstream tasks. Set the template for model compression that every production deployment of BERT-class models followed.
