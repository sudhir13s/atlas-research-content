## Why It Matters

Showed that prompting LLMs to produce intermediate reasoning steps before the final answer dramatically improves performance on math, logic, and multi-step problems. A simple prompting technique that unlocks capabilities the model already has. The most widely used prompting strategy in production — every serious LLM application uses some form of chain-of-thought.
