## Why It Matters

Defined five concrete categories of AI safety problems — safe exploration, side effects, reward hacking, scalable oversight, and distributional shift — grounding a previously abstract field in specific technical challenges. Written by researchers who later founded Anthropic. The paper that transformed AI safety from philosophy into an engineering discipline with tractable research problems.
