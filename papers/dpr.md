## Why It Matters

Showed that a simple dual-encoder trained on question-passage pairs dramatically outperforms BM25 for open-domain question answering. Proved that learned dense representations capture semantic similarity that keyword matching cannot. The paper that shifted the entire retrieval field from sparse to dense methods.
