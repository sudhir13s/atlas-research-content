## Summary

A paper presents a systematic evaluation framework applying locally served models to agentic power electronics tasks, measuring their power, energy, token, and time costs. The study characterizes the capability boundary of a local agent and the cost per solved task based on these measurements.

## Why It Matters

The results are expected to provide practical insight for the use of agentic AI in power electronics and the power systems it serves.
