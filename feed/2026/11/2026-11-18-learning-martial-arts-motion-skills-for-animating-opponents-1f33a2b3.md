## Summary

This paper presents a martial arts motion skills learning framework for character animation in virtual sparring games. The authors collected a motion capture dataset, pretrained a reusable repertoire of martial arts motion skills using reinforcement learning with adversarial style rewards, and adapted them for target-directed striking and threat-aware evasion. A VR sparring prototype for Meta Quest headsets demonstrates the applicability of these learned motions to interactive gameplay.
