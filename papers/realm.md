## Why It Matters

Jointly pretrained a language model and a knowledge retriever end-to-end, so the retriever learns what the language model needs. Unlike RAG which bolts retrieval onto a frozen model, REALM bakes retrieval into pretraining itself. Showed that grounding models in a retrievable knowledge store during pretraining improves factual accuracy.
