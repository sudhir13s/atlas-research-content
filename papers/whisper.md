## Why It Matters

Trained on 680,000 hours of multilingual audio with weak supervision, achieving near-human accuracy on English speech recognition without any fine-tuning. Works across 99 languages, handles accents, background noise, and technical jargon. Replaced the entire traditional ASR pipeline — now the default speech-to-text model for practitioners.
