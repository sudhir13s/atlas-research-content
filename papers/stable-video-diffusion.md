## Why It Matters

Fine-tuned Stable Diffusion on video data to create the first widely adopted open image-to-video model. Showed that image generation models can be efficiently adapted to video by adding temporal layers while preserving spatial quality. Became the foundation for an ecosystem of open video generation tools and custom animation workflows.
