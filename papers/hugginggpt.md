## Why It Matters

Used an LLM as a controller that decomposes complex requests, selects appropriate specialist models from Hugging Face, executes them, and synthesizes results. Showed that a single LLM can orchestrate hundreds of AI models as tools. An early and influential blueprint for multi-model agent systems and the task-decomposition pattern used in modern AI agents.
