## Why It Matters

State-of-the-art open text-to-video model released under Apache 2.0, generating high-quality videos with temporal consistency and camera control. Supports both text-to-video and image-to-video generation. The first open model to approach proprietary systems like Sora in quality, making advanced video generation accessible to researchers and developers.
