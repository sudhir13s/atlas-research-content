## Why It Matters

Introduced the concept of augmenting a seq2seq generator with a neural retriever that fetches relevant documents at generation time, grounding outputs in real sources. Reduced hallucination and enabled knowledge updates without retraining. The paper that coined 'RAG' and launched the most widely adopted pattern in production LLM applications.
