## Why It Matters

Scaled a vision-language model to 6B vision parameters with progressive alignment training, achieving GPT-4V-level performance on many benchmarks while being fully open-weight. Demonstrated that scaling the vision encoder matters as much as scaling the LLM. A key data point proving that open models can compete with proprietary multimodal systems.
