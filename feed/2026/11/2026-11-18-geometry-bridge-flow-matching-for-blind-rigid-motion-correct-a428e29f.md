## Summary

The authors present a 3D flow-matching prior approach called geometry-bridge flow matching for blind rigid-motion correction in head cone-beam CT. The method connects motion-corrupted and motion-free reconstructed images through progressively reduced simulated motion, utilizing analytic velocity targets from the forward projector's geometry derivative. Evaluations on simulated scans demonstrate state-of-the-art image reconstruction quality and motion accuracy compared to alternative methods.
