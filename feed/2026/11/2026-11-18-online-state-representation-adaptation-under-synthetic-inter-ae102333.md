## Summary

The paper tests state-representation adaptation in a one-step synthetic task by comparing an online updating logistic state predictor with a frozen predictor. Late action accuracy reached 93.39% with online updates compared to 64.63% with frozen coefficients. Additional comparisons with a direct policy and a contextual bandit highlight how feedback and action thresholds affect adaptation.
