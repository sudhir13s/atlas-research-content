## Why It Matters

Processes images at their native resolution using dynamic token allocation instead of forcing fixed crops, handling everything from thumbnails to high-res documents. Achieves state-of-the-art on document understanding, OCR, and visual QA benchmarks. The leading open-weight vision-language model, proving that resolution flexibility is key to practical multimodal AI.
