## Why It Matters

Unified score matching and diffusion models under a single framework of stochastic differential equations, providing a rigorous mathematical foundation for continuous-time generative modeling. Enabled exact likelihood computation and controllable generation through reverse-time SDEs. The theoretical backbone that made the entire diffusion model field rigorous.
