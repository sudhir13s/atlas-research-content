## Why It Matters

Framed video generation as world simulation — a diffusion transformer trained on variable-duration, resolution, and aspect-ratio videos that produces minute-long coherent scenes with 3D consistency. Demonstrated emergent understanding of physics, object permanence, and camera motion. Redefined the ambition for video generation from 'make clips' to 'simulate reality.'
