## Summary

The paper introduces RoboBuilder, a benchmark where coding agents co-design a robot's hardware and program from standard parts and 3D-printed components, with designs tested in both simulation and on a physical course. Agents use measurements and videos of real runs to update their designs and simulator parameters, successfully solving nearly all easy obstacles. When shown videos of their real failures, agents redesign the robots and remove 72% of level-1 and level-2 failures.
