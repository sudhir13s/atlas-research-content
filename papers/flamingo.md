## Why It Matters

Interleaved cross-attention layers between a frozen vision encoder and a frozen LLM, enabling few-shot multimodal learning from interleaved image-text sequences. Achieved strong performance with just a few examples, no fine-tuning needed. The first model to demonstrate that in-context learning extends to vision-language tasks, inspiring the entire VLM field.
