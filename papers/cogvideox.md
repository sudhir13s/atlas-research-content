## Why It Matters

Used a 3D VAE and expert transformer blocks to generate temporally consistent videos up to 6 seconds, with progressive training from low to high resolution. Open-source and reproducible, making it the most accessible research platform for video generation. Demonstrated that the DiT architecture scales cleanly from images to video with proper spatiotemporal attention.
