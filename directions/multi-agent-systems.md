## Why It Matters

Multi-agent systems have several AI agents work together or interact on a task, such as formalizing mathematics or exchanging messages and revising opinions. Recent papers introduce multi-agent frameworks for tasks like scaling proof libraries and formalizing research mathematics, while another evaluates the performance advantages and architectural gaps of automated multi-agent systems compared to single-agent baselines.
