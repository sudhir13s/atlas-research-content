## Why It Matters

Applied a pure transformer to video by dividing attention into separate spatial and temporal components, avoiding the quadratic cost of full spatiotemporal attention. Systematically compared five attention patterns and found divided attention optimal. The first successful video transformer, proving that the ViT paradigm extends to video understanding.
