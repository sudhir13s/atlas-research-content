## Why It Matters

Treats neural events and the relations among them with propositional logic, based on the “all-or-none” character of nervous activity.

- The authors find that the behaviour of every net can be described in these terms, with more complicated logical means for nets containing circles.
- For any logical expression satisfying certain conditions, a net can be found that behaves in the fashion it describes.
