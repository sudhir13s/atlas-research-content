## Why It Matters

A 1969 book by Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert analysing the computational capabilities of perceptrons for specific tasks.

- The MIT Press describes it as the first systematic study of parallelism in computation, with mathematical analysis showing the limitations of a class of computing machines that could be considered models of the brain.
- The publisher notes that research then turned away from the perceptron, perhaps as a result, and that the authors added a new chapter in 1987.
