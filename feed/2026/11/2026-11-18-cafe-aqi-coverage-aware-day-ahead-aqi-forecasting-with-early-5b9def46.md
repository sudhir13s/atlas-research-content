## Summary

This paper presents CAFE-AQI, a coverage-aware framework designed for next-day air quality forecasting, early warning generation, and conformal uncertainty estimation in Bangladesh. The framework incorporates temporal and pollutant features alongside a five-neighbor spatial context, utilizing trajectory weighting and risk-conditional conformal calibration. Evaluation on duplicate-balanced 2025 data shows an MAE of 9.76, an RMSE of 15.04, and an R-squared value of 0.910.
