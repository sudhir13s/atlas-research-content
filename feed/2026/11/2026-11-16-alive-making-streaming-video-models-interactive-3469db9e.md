## Summary

The authors introduce ALIVE, an interactive streaming video model that supports proactive monitoring, streaming task coordination, and feedback-seeking interaction. They construct ALIVE-SFT-288K and ALIVE-RL-3K for two-stage training, and introduce FSI-Bench to evaluate feedback seeking and subsequent task completion. ALIVE achieves the highest overall scores among the compared models on four public benchmarks and FSI-Bench, demonstrating strong streaming understanding and interactive capabilities.
