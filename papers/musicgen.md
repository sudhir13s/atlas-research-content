## Why It Matters

Generates high-quality music from text descriptions using a single autoregressive transformer over Encodec tokens, no cascading stages needed. Supports melody conditioning — hum a tune and get a full arrangement. The most accessible open text-to-music model, demonstrating that language model architectures transfer directly to audio generation.
