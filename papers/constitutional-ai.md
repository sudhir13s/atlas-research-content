## Why It Matters

Used a set of written principles to guide an AI in critiquing and revising its own outputs, then trained on the improved outputs — reducing the need for human feedback. Introduced the idea that AI can supervise its own alignment using constitutional rules. The methodology behind Claude's safety training and a scalable path to alignment as models grow beyond human evaluation capacity.
