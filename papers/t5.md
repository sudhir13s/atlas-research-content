## Why It Matters

Reframed every NLP task — classification, translation, summarization, QA — as a text-to-text problem with a single unified architecture. Accompanied by a massive empirical study comparing pretraining objectives, architectures, and datasets. Established the encoder-decoder design that powers Google Search and became the conceptual ancestor of instruction-following models.
