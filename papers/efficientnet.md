## Why It Matters

Discovered that uniformly scaling network depth, width, and resolution together using a compound coefficient yields much better accuracy/efficiency tradeoffs than scaling any dimension alone. EfficientNet-B7 achieved state-of-the-art ImageNet accuracy at 8.4x smaller than the best previous CNN. Set the standard for efficient architecture design before ViTs took over.
