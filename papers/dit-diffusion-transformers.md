## Why It Matters

Replaced the U-Net backbone in diffusion models with a standard transformer operating on latent patches. Showed that transformers scale better than U-Nets for image generation — quality improves predictably with compute. This is the architecture behind FLUX, Sora, and the current generation of frontier image and video models.
