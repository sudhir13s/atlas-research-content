## Why It Matters

Reframed audio generation as a language modeling problem over discrete audio tokens, generating coherent speech with realistic prosody and even piano music. Bridged the gap between NLP-style autoregressive modeling and raw audio. The conceptual foundation for all subsequent audio language models including VALL-E and MusicGen.
