## Summary

Researchers tested whether frozen bidirectional models captured subject-specific neuromuscular coupling between electromyographic activity and gait kinematics in 82 held-out able-bodied and 43 post-stroke participants. The study applied complete source-modality removal, cross-participant source substitution, and a template baseline. The findings showed that the model mainly relied on shared gait-phase patterns rather than clear subject-specific coupling.
