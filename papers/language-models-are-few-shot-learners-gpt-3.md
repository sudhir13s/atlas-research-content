## Why It Matters

Scaled to 175B parameters and discovered in-context learning — the ability to perform new tasks from a few examples in the prompt without any gradient updates. This was the moment the field realized that scale itself is an algorithm: capabilities emerge at size that simply do not exist in smaller models.
