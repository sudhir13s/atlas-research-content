## Why It Matters

Applied contrastive learning with masked prediction to raw audio, learning speech representations that dramatically reduce the labeled data needed for ASR — achieving good results with just 10 minutes of labeled speech. Proved that the BERT-style pretrain-then-fine-tune paradigm works for audio. The model that made self-supervised speech research mainstream.
