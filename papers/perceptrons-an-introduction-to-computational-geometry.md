## Why It Matters

Proved that single-layer perceptrons cannot learn XOR or other non-linearly-separable functions. This rigorous critique triggered the first AI winter — neural network funding dried up for 15 years. Ironic in hindsight, since multi-layer networks (which they acknowledged could work) eventually transformed the field.
