## Why It Matters

Applies back-propagation networks to handwritten digit recognition, using a highly constrained network architecture designed for the task and minimal preprocessing of the data.

On zip code digits provided by the U.S. Postal Service, the authors report a 1% error rate with about a 9% reject rate.
