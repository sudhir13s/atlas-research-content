## Why It Matters

Combined CLIP text embeddings with a diffusion prior and decoder to generate photorealistic images from text descriptions. Introduced the two-stage text-to-image architecture: first generate a CLIP image embedding from text, then decode it into pixels. Showed the world that AI could produce genuinely creative, high-quality imagery from natural language.
