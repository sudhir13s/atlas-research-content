## Why It Matters

Created a virtual town of 25 LLM-powered agents with memory streams, reflection, and planning — agents formed relationships, organized events, and exhibited emergent social behaviors. Demonstrated that believable human-like behavior emerges from the combination of memory retrieval, reflection synthesis, and planning. The most influential work on building persistent, socially intelligent AI agents.
