## Why It Matters

Introduced an end-to-end neural audio codec using residual vector quantization that compresses speech to 3kbps while maintaining quality suitable for real-time communication. The RVQ technique it pioneered became the standard tokenization method for all audio language models. Encodec, VALL-E, and MusicGen all descend directly from this work.
