## Why It Matters

Produced context-dependent word embeddings by running a bidirectional LSTM over the entire sentence, so the same word gets different representations depending on surrounding context. Solved the polysemy problem that Word2Vec and GloVe could not handle. The paper that kicked off the pretrained language model revolution — BERT and GPT are direct intellectual descendants.
