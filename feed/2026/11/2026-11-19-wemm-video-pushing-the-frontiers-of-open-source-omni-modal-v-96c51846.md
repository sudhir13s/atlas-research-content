## Summary

The authors introduce WeMM-Video, a family of Chinese-English bilingual omni-modal foundation models spanning 2B, 4B, 9B, and 35B-A3B variants. Evaluated across more than 120 benchmarks, the flagship WeMM-Video-35B-A3B model establishes a new open-source state of the art in omni-modal video evaluations.

## Why It Matters

The authors publicly release the WeMM-Video family along with their evaluation and reproduction pipeline to foster further research and development in the open-source community.
