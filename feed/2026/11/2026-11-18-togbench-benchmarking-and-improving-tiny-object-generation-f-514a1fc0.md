## Summary

The authors introduce TOGBench, a quantitative, object-level benchmark designed to evaluate tiny-object generation fidelity in aerial imagery. They note that existing generative models struggle with rendering small objects like vehicles, ships, and aircraft. To address this, they also propose two train- and inference-time techniques to improve layout-to-image generators.
