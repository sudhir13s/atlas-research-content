## Why It Matters

Replaced the entire traditional object detection pipeline — anchors, non-maximum suppression, hand-designed components — with a clean transformer encoder-decoder and bipartite matching loss. Proved that transformers can handle set prediction problems directly. Opened the door to end-to-end detection architectures that dominate today.
