## Why It Matters

Applied RLHF to GPT-3, training a reward model on human preferences and fine-tuning with PPO. A 1.3B InstructGPT was preferred by users over the 175B base GPT-3. Proved that alignment through human feedback matters more than raw scale for usability. The paper that made ChatGPT possible and launched the instruction-following era.
