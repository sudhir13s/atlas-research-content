## Why It Matters

Trained a monocular depth estimator on 62M images using a combination of labeled and pseudo-labeled data, producing zero-shot depth maps that rival dedicated sensors. Works on arbitrary images without fine-tuning. Essential for 3D scene understanding, robotics, and AR applications where LiDAR is unavailable.
