## Summary

The authors introduce Mind2Act World, a diagnostic benchmark designed to evaluate robotic manipulation across history-based cognitive reasoning, physical execution, and their composition. The benchmark includes 30 simulated tasks across five real-world domains with multiple difficulty levels and scene settings. Evaluations of multimodal agents, vision-language-action models, and world-action models reveal that competence in isolated views does not translate to success in combined tasks.

## Why It Matters

The benchmark provides a diagnosis harness that attributes failed episodes to reasoning, execution, or their composition, pointing to the integration of reasoning and execution through environmental feedback as the main open problem.
