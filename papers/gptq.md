## Why It Matters

Developed a one-shot post-training quantization method that compresses LLMs to 3-4 bits per weight using approximate second-order information, with minimal accuracy loss. Processes a 175B model in about 4 GPU-hours. Enabled running models like LLaMA-65B on consumer GPUs for the first time, catalyzing the local LLM movement.
