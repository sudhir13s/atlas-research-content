## Why It Matters

Proposed a two-stream architecture with co-attention between separate vision and language transformers, enabling each modality to attend to the other. One of the earliest works to apply transformers to joint vision-language understanding. Established the co-attention pattern and task formulations (VQA, image-text matching) that later VLMs refined and scaled.
