## Why It Matters

Comprehensive survey covering planning, memory, tool use, and multi-agent coordination patterns across the rapidly evolving LLM agent landscape. Provides a taxonomy that organizes dozens of agent papers into a coherent framework. Essential reading for anyone building agents — saves months of literature review by mapping the entire design space.
