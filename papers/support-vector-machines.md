## Why It Matters

A 1995 book by Vladimir N. Vapnik on the fundamental ideas behind the statistical theory of learning and generalization. The publisher lists among its topics:

- the empirical risk minimization principle and conditions for its consistency;
- non-asymptotic bounds on the risk it achieves;
- principles for controlling the generalization ability of learning machines with small samples;
- a new type of universal learning machine that controls generalization ability.
