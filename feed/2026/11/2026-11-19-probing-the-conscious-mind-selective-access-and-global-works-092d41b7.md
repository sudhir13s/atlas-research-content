## Summary

The authors introduce the Multimodal Concept Lens (MC-Lens) to study global workspace theory in vision-language models. The method maps intermediate states to a shared semantic space to compare concept accessibility across modalities and examine downstream computations. Experiments show that concept information is selectively accessible and participates in reasoning.

## Why It Matters

These findings offer an experimental basis to study GWT in multimodal artificial systems and how it can guide cross-modal alignment improvements.
