## Why It Matters

Showed that BERT was significantly undertrained and that simply training longer, on more data, with larger batches, and removing the next-sentence-prediction objective yields large gains. A masterclass in rigorous ablation studies. Proved that many 'architectural innovations' are actually just hyperparameter and training recipe improvements — a lesson the field keeps relearning.
