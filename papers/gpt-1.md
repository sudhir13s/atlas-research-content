## Why It Matters

First demonstration that generative pretraining on unlabeled text followed by discriminative fine-tuning produces strong NLP models. Established the GPT recipe: unsupervised pretraining gives you general language understanding, then a small amount of labeled data specializes it. The seed of the entire GPT lineage.
