## Why It Matters

Develops a theory for a hypothetical nervous system, the perceptron, to address how information about the physical world is sensed, remembered, and used in recognition and behaviour.

- Rosenblatt presents the theory as a bridge between biophysics and psychology.
- It allows learning curves to be predicted from neurological variables and vice versa.
