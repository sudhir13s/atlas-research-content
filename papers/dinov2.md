## Why It Matters

Self-supervised ViT trained on 142M curated images that produces universal visual features competitive with CLIP but without needing text supervision. The features transfer to depth estimation, segmentation, and retrieval out of the box. The default visual backbone when you need general-purpose image embeddings.
