## Why It Matters

Retrieves documents directly from page images using vision-language embeddings, bypassing the entire OCR-then-chunk-then-embed pipeline. Handles tables, figures, and complex layouts that text extraction destroys. A paradigm shift for document RAG — treat pages as images and let the model understand layout natively.
