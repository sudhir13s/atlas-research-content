## Why It Matters

Combined the global co-occurrence statistics of count-based methods with the linear substructure properties of Word2Vec, producing word vectors that excel at word analogy and similarity tasks. Showed that the implicit matrix factorization in Word2Vec and explicit co-occurrence counting are complementary approaches. GloVe vectors remain widely used as input features for classical NLP models.
