## Why It Matters

Added trainable copy layers to a frozen diffusion model that accept spatial conditions — edges, poses, depth maps — giving users precise control over generated image composition. Solved the 'I can't tell it where to put things' problem in image generation. Now a standard component in every serious image generation pipeline.
