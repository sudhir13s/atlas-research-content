## Summary

The authors introduce CurvDelta, a scalable framework that incorporates the preservation constraint into the parameterization of model updates by constructing a low-curvature space. They also present CurvLoRA, a low-rank variant designed for more efficient adaptation in tasks like capability learning and selective unlearning. Evaluations on mathematical reasoning, code generation, and knowledge unlearning show competitive performance while better preserving general capabilities.

## Why It Matters

Both methods achieve competitive downstream performance while better preserving general capabilities, offering a favorable acquisition--retention trade-off relative to the baselines.
