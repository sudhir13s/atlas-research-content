## Summary

The authors present EgoCaptioner for egocentric video captioning, which combines video-level overviews with timed steps whose narration adapts to perceptual demands. The system uses a coarse-to-fine annotation pipeline and Group Relative Policy Optimization guided by rewards that assess factual accuracy and temporal alignment. Across five egocentric benchmarks, EgoCaptioner achieves leading performance among evaluated open-source models in caption quality, caption-conditioned question answering, and temporal alignment.
