## Why It Matters

Reframed object detection as a single regression problem from image pixels to bounding boxes, running at 45 FPS versus the 7 FPS of two-stage detectors. Made real-time detection practical for the first time. YOLO and its descendants remain the default choice for production deployment where latency matters — robotics, autonomous vehicles, video analytics.
