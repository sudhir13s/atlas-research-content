## Why It Matters

A neural audio codec that compresses audio to extremely low bitrates using residual vector quantization while maintaining high fidelity. Produces the discrete audio tokens that MusicGen, AudioLM, and other audio language models operate over. The critical infrastructure layer that makes treating audio as a sequence of tokens possible.
