## Why It Matters

Scaled a dense transformer to 540B parameters across 6144 TPUs, demonstrating breakthrough performance on reasoning, code, and multilingual tasks. Showed that multi-query attention works at extreme scale. The engineering lessons on distributed training influenced every large model that followed.
