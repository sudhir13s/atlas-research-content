## Why It Matters

Proposed permutation language modeling — predicting tokens in random order to capture bidirectional context without the corruption of masked tokens that BERT uses. Outperformed BERT on 20 benchmarks at the time of release. Demonstrated that the masked LM objective was not the only way to achieve bidirectional pretraining, inspiring alternative training objectives.
